# Section 6: Responsible AI & Data Governance

**Purpose:** Documenting responsible AI principles and data classification rules within daily workplace operations.

## 1. Core Principles in Action

- **1. Accuracy:** Generative AI is treated as an initial drafting assistant. Every numerical figure, schedule date, and technical requirement is verified against ground-truth records.
- **2. Accountability:** The trainee (Saad Nasser) retains 100% ownership and legal accountability for all deliverables generated with AI assistance.
- **3. Confidentiality:** No proprietary project architecture, commercial agreements, or internal access credentials are ever inputted into public AI models.
- **4. Privacy:** Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as national IDs, phone numbers, and private employee data are completely anonymized or removed before prompting.
- **5. Bias Mitigation:** Reviewing AI-drafted evaluations and communications to ensure objective, neutral, and inclusive language across all stakeholder interactions.
- **6. Human Oversight:** AI never operates autonomously in decision-making; every workflow requires explicit human review and sign-off.

## 2. Workplace Data Classification Matrix (Green / Amber / Red)

| Data Tier | Sensitivity Level | Workplace Examples | Permitted AI Usage Guidelines |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Green** | **Public / Non-Sensitive** | Published project templates, public meeting agendas, general business email templates. | **Fully Permitted:** Can be processed using standard commercial AI models. |
| **Amber** | **Internal / Operational** | Internal sprint notes, milestone roadmaps, anonymized process flowcharts. | **Restricted / Anonymized:** Requires removal of project names, team identities, and specific dates before prompting. |
| **Red** | **Confidential / Restricted** | Employee performance records, financial budgets, server credentials, customer personal data. | **Strictly Prohibited:** NEVER input into external or non-enterprise Generative AI platforms. |

I hereby confirm that all data, prompts, and outputs contained within this GitHub repository are either completely synthetic, anonymized, or derived from open, non-sensitive educational lab exercises.
