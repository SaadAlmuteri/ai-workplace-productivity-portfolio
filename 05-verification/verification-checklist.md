# Section 5: Verification Protocol & Applied Audit

**Purpose:** Ensuring all AI-generated content is accurate, grounded, compliant, and verified before operational use.

## 1. Personal Verification Checklist (6-Point Protocol)

1. **Source Grounding:** Is every fact, date, name, and metric traceable to a verified primary source document?
2. **Hallucination Detection:** Did the AI generate any placeholder names, unapproved acronyms, or non-existent URLs/dates?
3. **Completeness & Gap Check:** Are missing inputs explicitly marked as `[Not Specified]` rather than filled with AI assumptions?
4. **Tone & Context Alignment:** Does the message match the organizational hierarchy and appropriate professional etiquette?
5. **Constraint Adherence:** Did the output strictly follow all length, format, and structural guidelines requested?
6. **Accountability Sign-off:** As the human owner, am I willing to stand behind the accuracy and consequences of this deliverable?

## 2. Worked Verification Example

**Artifact Audited:** Executive Status Brief (Section 2 Draft regarding cloud infrastructure budget).

| Verification Step | Audit Action Taken | Findings / Corrections | Status |

| **3. Tone Analysis** | Evaluated executive professionalism. | Removed alarming language ("platform will crash"); replaced with proactive planning language. | **Corrected** |
| **4. Constraint Check** | Verified word count and formatting structure. | Output complies with 3-bullet executive summary format. | **Verified** |
| **5. Human Sign-off** | Final review by Project Coordinator. | Document signed off by Saad Nasser Al-Mutairi. | **Approved** |
