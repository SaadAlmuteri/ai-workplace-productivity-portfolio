# Section 7: Personal AI Integration Plan

**Role:** Project Coordinator  
**Objective:** Embedding generative AI routines into recurring weekly tasks to maximize efficiency and communication quality.

## Recurring AI Workplace Workflows

### 1. Weekly Meeting Synthesis & Action Tracking
- **Expected Benefit:** Reduces time spent drafting meeting minutes and action registers from 45 minutes to 10 minutes per sync.
- **Preferred Prompt / Workflow:** `03-information-workflow` (R.C.T.O. extraction prompt with `[Not Specified]` constraints).
- **Key Risk & Verification:** Risk of missing subtle verbal agreements; mitigated by cross-referencing action items with the meeting recording.
- **Frequency of Use:** 2–3 times per week after major project standups.
- **Definition of Success:** Meeting minutes distributed within 30 minutes of meeting adjournment with 100% task owner clarity.

### 2. Stakeholder Milestone Updates & Correspondence
- **Expected Benefit:** Accelerates drafting of diplomatic, professional progress updates and change notifications while maintaining executive tone.
- **Preferred Prompt / Workflow:** `02-writing-workflow` (4-Step Drafting & Tone Refinement Workflow).
- **Key Risk & Verification:** Potential hallucination of delivery dates or budget numbers; verified against the official project Jira/Smartsheet board.
- **Frequency of Use:** Weekly on Thursdays prior to executive leadership updates.
- **Definition of Success:** Clear, zero-error status briefs delivered consistently with positive stakeholder feedback on clarity.

### 3. Sprint Planning & Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)
- **Expected Benefit:** Rapidly transforms high-level operational goals into structured phases, sub-tasks, and risk registers.
- **Preferred Prompt / Workflow:** `04-planning-workflow` (The Planning Chain: Goal → Mechanisms → Phases → Tasks).
- **Key Risk & Verification:** AI might generate generic, non-actionable tasks; resolved by applying domain-specific operational constraints.
- **Frequency of Use:** Bi-weekly at the start of every sprint planning cycle.
- **Definition of Success:** Complete sprint task boards initialized and assigned 50% faster than manual setup.
