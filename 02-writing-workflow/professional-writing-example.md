# Section 2: Executive Writing Workflow (4-Step Process)

**Workplace Task:** Communicating a Project Change Request to Executive Stakeholders.

### Step 1: AI-Generated Draft
*Prompt: "Draft an email requesting a 10% budget expansion for cloud infrastructure due to increased user load."*

> **Initial AI Output:**  
> Subject: Budget Increase  
> Hello, we need 10% more budget for cloud servers because we have more users now. It will cost around $15,000 extra. If we don't get this, the platform will crash soon. Please approve this as soon as possible. Regards.

### Step 2: Fact Verification
- **Budget Figures Checked:** Verified against actual vendor quotes ($12,500 SAR, not $15,000 USD).
- **Technical Metric Verified:** Traffic increase confirmed at 35% growth over 30 days based on analytics logs.
- **Tone & Risk Assessment:** Removed alarmist phrasing ("platform will crash soon") and substituted with proactive capacity planning.

### Step 3: Tone Refinement
- Adjusted tone from demanding ("approve as soon as possible") to collaborative, data-backed executive justification.
- Structured the request into clear business impacts: Cause → Data Evidence → Solution & Financial Breakdown → Next Steps.

### Step 4: Human Review & Sign-off
- **Reviewer:** Saad Nasser Al-Mutairi (Project Coordinator)
- **Approved Date:** September 2026
- **Status:** Approved for Executive Transmission

### Final Human-Approved Version

> **Subject:** Project CloudScale — Infrastructure Capacity Adjustment Request  
> 
> **Dear Project Steering Committee,**  
> 
> Over the past 30 days, active user volume on the pilot platform has grown by **35%**, surpassing our initial baseline projections. To maintain optimal system latency and ensure uninterrupted service availability, the technical operations team recommends a minor expansion of our cloud infrastructure tier.  
> 
> **Financial & Technical Overview:**  
> - **Current Monthly Allocation:** 25,000 SAR  
> - **Proposed Adjustment:** +2,500 SAR / month (10% capacity expansion)  
> - **Key Deliverables:** Auto-scaling database cluster and enhanced data backup redundancy.  
> 
> This adjustment remains within our overall contingency reserve for Phase 2. Detailed capacity logs and vendor pricing sheets are available for your review.  
> 
> We welcome your formal approval by Thursday to initiate provisioning before the upcoming monthly sprint cycle.  
> 
> Sincerely,  
> **Saad Nasser Al-Mutairi**  
> *Project Coordinator | Operations Division*
