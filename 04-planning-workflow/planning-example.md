# Section 4: Planning Workflow (The Planning Chain)

**Workplace Scenario:** Coordinating the Rollout of a New Internal Digital Task Management System across 3 Operational Departments.

## 1. Goal Definition
**Goal:** Successfully transition 45 team members from manual email tracking to a centralized digital project management workspace within 4 weeks, achieving 100% active adoption and zero lost operational deliverables.

## 2. Mechanisms
- **Structured Onboarding:** Weekly hands-on training sessions with departmental leads.
- **Workflow Templates:** Standardized project templates to eliminate manual setup friction.
- **Feedback & Support Desk:** Daily 30-minute office hours during initial rollout weeks.

## 3. Execution Phases & Actionable Tasks

### Phase 1: Workspace Architecture & Setup (Week 1)
- [x] **Task 1.1:** Configure workspace hierarchy (Projects, Folders, Sprint Boards).
- [x] **Task 1.2:** Establish role-based permission access (Admin, Member, Guest).
- [x] **Task 1.3:** Create 3 standardized workflow templates (Operations, IT, Admin).

### Phase 2: Pilot Testing & Key User Training (Week 2)
- [ ] **Task 2.1:** Onboard 6 department champions for a 3-day pilot dry run.
- [ ] **Task 2.2:** Gather usability feedback and refine custom metadata fields.
- [ ] **Task 2.3:** Conduct live 45-minute training workshops for all department staff.

### Phase 3: Live Migration & Cut-Over (Week 3)
- [ ] **Task 3.1:** Freeze legacy tracking spreadsheets.
- [ ] **Task 3.2:** Migrate all active open tickets into the new digital board.
- [ ] **Task 3.3:** Host daily virtual support office hours for user questions.

### Phase 4: Audit, Optimization & Sign-off (Week 4)
- [ ] **Task 4.1:** Audit task completion rates and identify inactive accounts.
- [ ] **Task 4.2:** Publish "Best Practices & FAQ Guide" in the team repository.
- [ ] **Task 4.3:** Present 30-day adoption metrics report to Operations Leadership.
